Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

PLAN is a minimalist multi-list to-do app for iOS that presents one focused list at a time. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. PLAN has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. There is no server of ours to send data to.

Where your data lives

All lists, list items, themes, and preferences are stored on your device, using Apple's SwiftData and UserDefaults frameworks. When your device is signed into iCloud, they also sync to your personal iCloud account so your lists stay current across your devices (see below). Nothing is ever sent to us.

iCloud

PLAN syncs your lists and settings between your own Apple devices through your personal iCloud account — Apple's CloudKit private database and iCloud key-value storage. This data is stored in your iCloud, counts against your iCloud storage, and is accessible only to you and Apple, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no server, no access, and no copy. If your device isn't signed into iCloud, PLAN simply works locally — sync stays idle and nothing leaves the device.

Widgets

PLAN offers optional Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets that show your next unfinished task and let you mark it done with a tap. The app shares your task data with the widget through a private, on-device App Group container — not accessible to other apps. Because widgets render on your Home and Lock Screen, your task titles may be visible there, including (for Lock Screen widgets) while your device is locked. The widget collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no network access of its own.

AI features

The current version of PLAN exposes no AI features. The codebase contains a title-summarization capability that is not reachable from the app's interface, and even that runs against a local stub that sends nothing anywhere. If a future version enables AI features or routes them through a remote model, this policy will be updated and you will be notified before any data leaves your device.

Children

PLAN is rated for ages 4+. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we collect nothing, there is no data to access, export, or delete on our side. Uninstalling the app deletes all PLAN data stored on that device. Data synced to your iCloud account remains under your control there — you can remove it in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or by clearing your lists in the app before uninstalling (deletions sync to your other devices).
